Questions people ask

How do I cancel Winkaboo Unlimited?
  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top (Apple ID), then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Winkaboo and tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Winkaboo Unlimited until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I cancel the free trial so I’m not charged?

Cancel it the same way, at least 24 hours before the 3-day trial ends. Otherwise it converts to the paid yearly plan.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the same Apple ID you bought with, open Winkaboo and tap Restore Purchases on the Winkaboo Unlimited screen. If it doesn’t come back, email us.

How do the free shots work?

Winkaboo is free with 5 shots a day, and the count starts over each day. Winkaboo Unlimited removes the limit and adds Pro picks.

Where are my photos kept?

On your phone, in Winkaboo’s gallery. Best frames stay until you delete them; backups and the short clip of each moment are kept for 30 days. A photo goes into your Photos library only when you ask. Winkaboo’s own copies are not in device backups, so deleting the app deletes them. Save what you want to keep to Photos first.

How do I delete a photo?

Open the gallery in Winkaboo and delete a photo, a burst or a whole shoot. This removes it from the app, not from Photos. To remove a photo from Photos, delete it there.

Is my camera feed uploaded?

No. Winkaboo reads the camera on your phone. Only Pro picks (Winkaboo Unlimited) sends photos off your phone, and only the frames of a burst, to choose the best one. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
