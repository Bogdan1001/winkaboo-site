Photos that take themselves.

Winkaboo is a camera with a tarsier friend inside. It shows you the pose, waits for your moment and clicks the shutter itself, so you can be in the photo instead of behind it.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

1

It shows the pose.

Pick a pose and Winkaboo shows you how to strike it, then guides your framing until it looks right.

2

It waits for your moment.

When you hit the pose, Winkaboo takes the shot by itself, a quick burst so the right frame is in there.

3

It keeps your best.

From every burst it keeps the best frame, leaves out the near-copies and the blinks, and cuts your shoot into a reel.
