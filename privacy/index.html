Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 (2026-10-29)

The short version

  • Winkaboo reads the camera on your phone. The live camera feed is not uploaded.
  • Your photos stay on your phone, in Winkaboo, until you delete them. They go to Photos only when you ask.
  • Cloud picks (Winkaboo Unlimited only, where the app offers them, and only if you turn them on) are the one feature that sends photos off your phone: the frames of a shot go to Winkaboo’s servers, where they are evaluated — including by third-party AI providers — to choose the best one, and we don’t keep them afterwards.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

Winkaboo is made by Cyberfruit (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: hi@cyberfruit.ai.

2. The camera

Winkaboo needs the camera. It looks at the live picture on your phone to find the person in frame, read the pose and the framing, and check faces for things like open eyes, which way they face and sharpness, so it knows the right moment. This analysis happens on the device. The App Store version of Winkaboo does not upload, record or log what the camera sees. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings › Winkaboo.

3. Your photos on your phone

  • Each time Winkaboo takes a shot, it keeps the best frame and up to two backups in Winkaboo’s own storage on your phone, along with a short clip of the seconds before the catch.
  • Best frames stay until you delete them. Backups and the short clip are kept for 30 days, unless you saved them to Photos.
  • Reels are made on your phone from your good photos and kept with the shoot until you delete it.
  • Winkaboo’s copies are stored only on your phone and are not part of device backups, so deleting the app deletes them. You can delete photos, shoots and reels inside Winkaboo at any time; that removes them from the app, not from Photos.

4. Saving to Photos

Winkaboo saves a photo or video to your Photos library only when you ask it to. It asks for “add only” access, which lets it add to your library but not look through it.

5. Cloud picks (Winkaboo Unlimited only)

If you have Winkaboo Unlimited, you can turn on Cloud picks where the app offers them: the app asks you the first time you press “Pick the best”, and you can switch between Cloud and On this iPhone any time in Settings. When it is on, the frames of each shot are sent over an encrypted connection to Winkaboo’s servers, where they are evaluated — including by third-party AI providers — to choose the best frame. The frames are sent only to make that choice, and Winkaboo does not keep them afterwards. With Cloud picks off, or without Winkaboo Unlimited, no photo leaves your phone.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Winkaboo learns only whether your subscription is active; when Cloud picks are on, the app shows our server Apple’s signed confirmation of that subscription, so the server can check it. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

7. What we don’t do

  • No accounts: you don’t sign up or give us your name or email to use Winkaboo.
  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics software from other companies in the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell your data.

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated usage and crash reports if you have chosen to share them with developers in your iPhone’s settings.

8. This website

This site has no cookies, trackers, analytics, or fonts and scripts from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement.

9. Your choices and rights

You can delete your photos in the app, turn off camera and Photos access in Settings, and cancel your subscription with Apple. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion; email us and we’ll help.

10. Children

Winkaboo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects what we collect, we will say so in the app or on this site.
